Hi, I’m Mehrad.

I’m a mixed reality researcher and engineer based in Calgary. I’m finishing my BSc in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, where I started doing research with Prof. Ryo Suzuki in the Programmable Reality Lab. Since then I’ve spent four years as a mixed reality developer and researcher at MaKami College, and a year as a visiting researcher with Prof. Hideaki Kuzuoka in the Cyber Interface Lab at the University of Tokyo.

I build mixed reality systems that help people teach and learn physical, hands-on skills, especially at a distance. My work combines interaction techniques with live 3D capture, robots and haptics, and I test these systems with real instructors and learners.

I’m applying to MSc programs for Fall 2027, and I’m open to XR and engineering roles, including internships for Summer 2027.

Tools: Unity (C#) · Swift / visionOS · three.js · Meta Quest 3 · HoloLens 2 · Apple Vision Pro · Azure Kinect · SenseGlove Nova · ROS 2 · Python · JavaScript · Arduino · Figma

Selected work

Mixed reality that helps people hands-on work.

Teaching hands-on skills remotely

Massage, physiotherapy and other hands-on trades are taught in person. These systems let an instructor see, guide and reach students who aren’t in the room.

CHI 2023

ChameleonControlteach

A remote instructor guides a real person in the classroom through mixed reality gestures, so that person’s hands carry out the instructor’s demonstration. Deployed in a physiotherapy classroom and evaluated with 53 participants across three studies.

UCalgary
AHs 2026 · IEEE VR 2026 Workshop

Scalable Academysupervise

A VR view that streams live 3D pointclouds of up to six students at once. In a study with eight massage therapy experts, the bottleneck shifted from walking the classroom to managing attention, and a model of the results predicts one instructor could monitor up to 15 students.

University of TokyoCU Boulder
UIST 2023

HoloBotsshare

Mobile robots give a remote person’s hologram a physical presence, so collaborators in different places can touch and move the same real objects.

University of TsukubaUCalgary

Turning instructions into guidance

Most how-to knowledge lives in flat videos and conversation. These projects bring it into the space where the work happens.

IUI 2025

Video2MRlearn

Extracts motion from ordinary 2D tutorial videos and turns it into 3D mixed reality instructions you can follow in your own space.

Adobe ResearchCarnegie MellonUniversity of TsukubaUCalgary
UIST 2022

Sketched Realityprototype

Sketch in AR to define how virtual objects and small tabletop robots affect each other, in both directions.

Augmented Conversation

AR that listens to a conversation and places relevant keywords, maps, photos and facts around the person you’re talking to, so you don’t have to reach for your phone.
