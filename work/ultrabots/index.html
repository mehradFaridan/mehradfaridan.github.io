UltraBots: Large-Area Mid-Air Haptics for VR with Robotically Actuated Ultrasound Transducers

UIST 2022 Student Innovation ContestHonorable MentionHapticsVirtual realityRobotics

Mehrad Faridan, Marcus Friedel, Ryo Suzuki

PaperPDFVideo

Ultrasound haptics can create precise touch sensations in mid-air, but each device covers only a small area, which limits what you can do with it in VR. UltraBots mounts ultrasound transducer arrays on tabletop mobile robots (Sony Toio) or robotic arms. Using the headset’s hand tracking, the robots keep the arrays under the user’s hands, giving on-demand mid-air haptics across a much larger workspace. We demonstrated applications in virtual workspaces, medical training, education and entertainment.
