HoloBots: Augmenting Holographic Telepresence with Mobile Robots for Tangible Remote Collaboration in Mixed Reality

UIST 2023Mixed realityTelepresenceTabletop robots

Keiichi Ihara, Mehrad Faridan, Ayumi Ichikawa, Ikkaku Kawaguchi, Ryo Suzuki

PaperPDFVideo

Mixed reality telepresence can make a remote person visible in your space, but their hologram can’t touch anything. HoloBots pairs a remote user’s live hologram (HoloLens 2 and Azure Kinect) with small tabletop robots (Sony Toio) that move in sync with their hands, so the remote person can push, grasp and move real objects next to a local collaborator. We explored a design space that includes object actuation, physicalized virtual hands, world-in-miniature exploration, shared tangible interfaces, embodied guidance and haptic communication. In a study with twelve participants, HoloBots significantly increased co-presence and the sense of a shared experience compared with hologram-only and robot-only conditions.
