PHINIX: Perceptive Helper with Intelligent Navigation and Intuitive eXperience

Ximira · CES 2024Best of CES 2024Assistive technologyWearablesHaptics

Mehrad Faridan, Haptics Engineer (Part-time) at Ximira, 2023 – 2024

Website Video
PHINIX photo coming soon

PHINIX is an Intel-funded AI wearable from Ximira that helps blind and low-vision people navigate, read text and recognize faces on their own. It turns visual information around the wearer into audio and haptic feedback.

My role

I designed the audio and voice interface, wrote the module that prioritizes what the device tells the user, and prototyped parts for the haptic bracelet.

Recognition

PHINIX won PC Pro’s Best of CES 2024.
