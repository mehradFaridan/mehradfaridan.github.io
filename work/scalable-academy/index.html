Towards Scalable Remote Hands-on Education: Evaluating a Multi-Student Pointcloud Interface for Massage Therapy Education

AHs 2026 Poster· IEEE VR 2026 Workshop (VR-HSA)Virtual realityPoint cloudsRemote education

Mehrad Faridan*, Takashige Suzuki*, Takafumi Kanaya, Ryo Suzuki, Hideaki Kuzuoka* Equal contribution

PaperPDF
Project image coming soon

In massage therapy classes, an instructor has to watch each student’s hands on a real body, which makes remote teaching hard to scale. Scalable Academy streams live 3D point clouds of students, captured with Azure Kinect depth cameras, into a single VR view so one instructor can supervise a whole classroom from a distance. The instructor can switch between a scalability mode that shows up to six students at once and a fidelity mode for a closer look at one student. In a study with eight massage therapy instructors, participants projected that one instructor could oversee up to 15 students, and the bottleneck shifted away from the instructor’s physical workload. An earlier version, “Scalable Academy: Pointcloud Interface for Remote Hands-on Classroom”, was presented at the IEEE VR 2026 VR-HSA workshop.
